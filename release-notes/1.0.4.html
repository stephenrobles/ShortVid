Faster MP4s

  • Force MP4 no longer re-encodes video your Mac can already play. H.264, HEVC and, on M3 and later Macs, AV1 are repackaged into an MP4 in seconds; VP9 and anything else is still converted to H.264. On an M3 or later Mac, Highest quality now brings down YouTube's 4K AV1 without a conversion.
  • A new Compatible MP4 (H.264) option, off by default, converts AV1 too for files headed to older Macs, iPhones before the 15 Pro and other devices.
