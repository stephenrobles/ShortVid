• First release: a Download Video action for Shortcuts on the Mac. Give it a link from YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Instagram, X or any of the hundreds of sites yt-dlp supports, or a direct link to a media file, and it passes the video file to the next action.
  • Quality parameter: Highest quality, 1080p, 720p or 480p.
  • Force MP4 (on by default) delivers an H.264 MP4 that QuickTime and Photos play, remuxing when the streams already fit and converting with ffmpeg's hardware encoder when they don't.
  • Update yt-dlp… in the app fetches the latest yt-dlp release, verifies it, and uses it from then on, so a site change never needs a new version of the app.
  • Bundles yt-dlp, ffmpeg and Deno inside the app, signed and notarized. Requires macOS 27 or later and an Apple silicon Mac.
